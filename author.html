XXXX BLANK — THE WRITER

AUTOPILOT began as a test.

How far could I push myself? Could I create characters so disturbing, so utterly unlike the man I've become, that readers would believe they existed beyond the page?

The short answer: yes, I could, but it came with a heavy cost to my sanity. I put myself into the minds of the characters in order to create authentic voices.

Over the next few years I plan to explore more of the universe I've created, with at least ten more books planned, and a standing appointment with our local psychiatrist.
